## Key Summary & Observations

!!! warning "Special Note: Business Hour FRT Target"
    32 cases missed the 2-hour response time during business hours (excluding 60 cases outside office hours/weekends), resulting in a **7.84%** miss rate out of 408 valid cases. 
    
	**Action Required:** Staff are urged to seek immediate capacity support when occupied with complex phone tickets to better manage the email queue.

* **Volume Trend (February vs. March):** YTD cases reached 468. February recorded 127 cases, experiencing a drop due to the festive season. As of March 30, March has 136 closed and pending cases, comfortably exceeding February's total.
* **FRT Target Performance:** First Response Time (FRT) < 2hrs achieved 80.34%, maintaining performance just above the 80% corporate target.
* **NPS & Customer Satisfaction:** Customer satisfaction remains exceptionally high, far exceeding the 60.0 benchmark. NPS stands at 84.9 with a CES of 6.8, based on 33 surveys.
* **Additional Operational Metrics:** Knowledge Reuse reached **12.61%**, Resolution under 3 days was **38.03%**, and the Escalation Rate was kept low at **2.37%** across 185 unique accounts.
