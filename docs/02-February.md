## Key Summary & Observations

!!! warning "Special Note: Business Hour FRT Target"
    19 cases missed the 2-hour response time during business hours, resulting in a **6.21%** miss rate, exceeding our 5% tolerance. 
    
	**Action Required:** Staff are urged to seek immediate capacity support when occupied with complex phone tickets to better manage the email queue.

* **Seasonal Volume Dip:** YTD cases reached 306, a slight YoY drop of -0.14%. February volume dropped due to the festive season. A March volume spike is forecasted due to MFA implementation.
* **FRT Target Met:** First Response Time (FRT) < 2hrs achieved 81.05%, maintaining performance above the 80% corporate target. Speed performance decreased slightly from January's 82.4%.
* **Excellent NPS Score:** Customer satisfaction remains exceptionally high, far exceeding the 60.0 benchmark. NPS stands at 92.9 with a CES of 6.9, based on 14 surveys.
* **Channel & Product Mix:** Email dominance continues at 86.93% (266 cases). Tekla Structural Designer (143 cases) and Tekla Structures (125 cases) ratio returned to the standard 60:40.
