    Key Summary & Observations

    Special Note: Business Hour FRT Target

    39 cases missed the 2-hour response time during business hours, resulting in a 6.12% miss rate out of 637 valid cases.

    Action Required: Staff are urged to seek immediate capacity support when occupied with complex phone tickets to better manage the email queue.

    • Volume Trend (March vs. April): YTD cases reached 691. April recorded 168 cases, experiencing a slight decrease compared to last month. As of April 27, April has 168 cases, slightly lower than March's total of 186.
    • FRT Target Performance: First Response Time (FRT) < 2hrs achieved 80.61%, maintaining performance just above the 80% corporate target.
    • NPS & Customer Satisfaction: Customer satisfaction remains exceptionally high, far exceeding the 60.0 benchmark. NPS stands at 88.9 with a CES of 6.8, based on 54 surveys.
    • Additional Operational Metrics: Knowledge Reuse reached 14.76%, Resolution under 3 days was 40.09%, and the Escalation Rate was kept low at 1.75% across 238 unique accounts.
